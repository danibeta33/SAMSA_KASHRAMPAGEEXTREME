## How the bonus features work in Dead Heat

### Bluff's Shot

On every base game round, Bluff takes a shot in parallel with the reels. When
it drops, one modifier hits the court before the round settles:

- **WILD RAIN**: extra wilds land on the court.
- **SPIN MULT**: the whole round is multiplied by 2x, 3x or 5x.
- **LOW SWEEP**: low symbols are cleared and refilled.

### Rival cards

Land **3 or more cards of the SAME rival** to start that rival's shootout.
Mixed cards do not trigger. 4 cards start you one point further ahead; 5 or
more start you two points ahead with a 2x multiplier and a guaranteed modifier.

### The Shootout

A one-on-one chase. Bluff starts ahead (2 points against Ash, 4 against
Watts, 6 against Otto) and both keep scoring. The feature **ends when the
rival ties the score**, so every possession you stay ahead is another chance
to build the win.

Every Bluff basket grants a buff from that rival's list. Every rival basket
gives one of two things, never both: either that rival's signature play, or
**CROWD RALLY**, which adds +2 to the global multiplier, a buff you only
enjoy while you survive.

- **+N HEAT**: adds N to the global multiplier.
- **WILD RAIN / FAST BREAK**: extra wilds land on the court.
- **WILD SLAM**: a block of wilds lands on the court.
- **QUICK HANDS**: upgrades low symbols to mid ones.
- **INTEL**: upgrades mid symbols to high ones.
- **BACKBOARD BREAK**: clears symbols and refills them.
- **CAMERA HACK**: turns a court camera into a permanent wild cell for the
  rest of the chase.
- **EXTRA SHOT**: Bluff shoots again in the same possession.

Inside the shootout, the Tumble Multiplier resets on every possession. The
global multiplier is the one that carries across the whole chase.

**Sudden death:** each rival has a possession limit of 28 for Ash, 35 for
Watts and 45 for Otto. On that possession the rival scores for certain, so the
feature always ends.
