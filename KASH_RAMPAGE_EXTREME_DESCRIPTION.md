## How the bonus features work in Kash Rampage Extreme

### KASH RAMPAGE

The signature mechanic. On any base game spin, and on any Free Spin of the
three buy modes, Kash can smash the board: **every Low and Mid symbol on the
grid is converted** into a High symbol (85% chance per cell) or into KASH, the
Premium symbol (15% chance per cell).

- Conversions land on the initial drop, before clusters are detected, and are
  visible on the board.
- Scatters and Wilds are never converted.
- KASH RAMPAGE strikes at most once per spin and never during tumbles.
